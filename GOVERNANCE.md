# Governance

WarHound AI maintains HoundBrowser. Maintainers review contributions, prioritize issues, manage compatibility, and publish releases. Technical decisions should be explained in issues or pull requests so contributors can understand their tradeoffs.

Contributions do not require a commercial WarHound account. The Apache 2.0 license permits independent use and forks subject to its terms. The WarHound and HoundBrowser names and visual marks are not separately licensed trademarks by this repository.
