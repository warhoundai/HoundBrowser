# Community conduct

Help make HoundBrowser useful and welcoming. Discuss ideas and behavior, not personal attributes. Give constructive reviews, credit others' work, respect privacy, and do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately using the WarHound account owner's contact channel listed at https://warhoundai.com. Reports are handled with discretion; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when legal or safety obligations apply.
